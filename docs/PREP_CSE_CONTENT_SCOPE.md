# Prep Studio CSE study-pack scope and verification

**Status:** Curator-authored candidate content for Prep Studio. This is not an official VTU publication, a complete CSE degree syllabus, or a guarantee that a particular college uses this scheme. Students should compare the course codes, option choices, lab instructions and exam scope with their current college handout.

## What is in the CSE seed pack

The CSE-specific seed contains **21 study-pack records, 105 five-part study units and 105 module-linked practice questions**. Each study unit is rendered in the existing Prep format: an explanation, subtopic briefs, a key model, a worked application, an exam tip, a solve method and a question. The module/unit IDs describe the current content, not the university's paper-question IDs.

The candidate map includes:

| Coverage | Included packs |
| --- | --- |
| First-year programming foundation | Principles of Programming Using C (currently tagged BPOPS103; semester/code placement needs confirmation) |
| Semester 3 | BCS301–304, the separate BCSL305 Data Structures Lab, and the two BCS306 language alternatives |
| Semester 4 | BCS401 Analysis and Design of Algorithms, BCS402 Microcontrollers, BCS403 Database Management Systems |
| Semester 5 | BCS501 Software Engineering and Project Management, BCS502 Computer Networks, BCS503 Theory of Computation, and the separate BCSL504 Web Technology Lab |
| Semester 6 | BCS601 Cloud Computing and BCS602 Machine Learning |
| Semester 7 | BCS701 Internet of Things, BCS702 Parallel Computing and BCS703 Cryptography and Network Security |
| Supplemental, unsemestered | Mathematical Foundations for Computing; it makes no VTU course-code or semester claim |

The seed route also adds the existing cross-program Student Toolkit and Science Reasoning supplement to CSE. Those two supplementary packs are not CSE university courses.

### Options and integrated practical work

- **BCS306A (Java) and BCS306B (C++) are alternatives.** The VTU CSE scheme says to choose the option that is not similar to the programming course taken in first year. Both are present as study aids; a student should use only the registered option. Their module detail is cross-referenced to the sibling CSD syllabus, not directly confirmed against a CSE detailed syllabus.
- **BCSL305 and BCSL504 are separate lab courses** and are represented as five Prep study blocks each. Those blocks organize experiments for learning; they are not claimed to be official theory modules.
- **BCS302 and BCS303 remain single IPCC course records.** Their lab/practical work must be treated as integrated course components, not extra standalone courses. BCS304 remains its own separate course. The precise current BCS302/303 experiment lists still need the college's CSE lab sheet.
- BCS502's directly listed practical component is included within the BCS502 course topics; it has not been split into another course. Other IPCC practical components are not yet transcribed exhaustively into their theory modules.
- BCS404 Analysis and Design of Algorithms Lab appears in the official semester-4 scheme but is **not yet a seed pack**: a detailed CSE experiment list was not verified. Do not infer that it is covered by the BCS401 theory pack.

## Explicit omissions and limits

This is **not complete four-year CSE coverage**. It deliberately omits:

- Semester 2 and most first-year mathematics, science, engineering and common courses.
- Semester 3 common/AEC/SEC/social-connect requirements and any option not represented above.
- Semester 4 BCS404 lab, BCS405x electives, AEC/SEC choices and other scheme requirements.
- Semester 5 professional-elective choices (BCS515x), Mini Project (BCS586), Research Methodology/IPR, Environmental Studies/E-waste, mandatory common courses and other requirements.
- Semester 6 professional/open electives and other common or scheme requirements.
- Semester 7 professional/open electives and Major Project Phase II.
- Semester 8 and any later regulation or college-specific additions.
- Exhaustive practical experiment lists for every IPCC course, instructor notes, official model answers, and prior-year question papers. The BCS401 model paper informed topic grouping only; it is not included as a PYQ pack.

Accordingly, “all modules/topics/subtopics” means all study units and subtopic briefs currently authored in these **selected candidate packs**, not every item in the complete CSE degree or every local lab manual. The CSE pack is marked as a candidate VTU 2022-aligned core so it can be reviewed and expanded without implying official endorsement or full-scheme coverage.
